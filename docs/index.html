Homebrew for the Wii U

Everything you watch and listen to, on your Wii U.

CoffeeFlix brings YouTube, Jellyfin, Twitch, internet radio, podcasts and your own videos, music, photos and books together in one fast app made for the TV and the GamePad. No accounts, no ads, nothing to set up.

One app for your couch

Built for the Wii U from the ground up: hardware video decoding, smooth animations, and controls that work the same on the GamePad, Pro Controller, Wii Remote and the touch screen.

YouTube, without an account

A For you feed that learns what you like on the console itself. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Subscriptions with a newest-first feed, imported from Google Takeout or NewPipe
  • Channel pages with videos, Shorts, live streams and playlists
  • Watch later, history, saved playlists and captions
  • Skips sponsor segments with SponsorBlock

Jellyfin

Your own movie and TV server, with continue watching, next up, seasons, subtitles and resume that syncs back. Sign in with Quick Connect.

Twitch

Live channels, categories, search and a follow list, at the quality you choose (60 fps optional).

Radio

Over 40,000 stations from radio-browser.info, by country and genre, with favorites and live "now playing" titles.

Podcasts

Search the Apple Podcasts directory and top charts, subscribe to shows, and pick up episodes where you left off.

Your own media

Videos with thumbnails and subtitles, music with cover art, photos and slideshows, and comics (CBZ) and EPUB books, from the SD card.

Network & media servers

Play straight from a PC, Mac or NAS share (SMB), and from Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and other DLNA servers found automatically.

Search everything

One search across YouTube, Jellyfin, Twitch, radio and podcasts, and a Home screen that pulls it all together.

Install

You need a Wii U with the Aroma homebrew environment. If you haven't set it up yet, the guide takes you through it step by step.

Download

Get coffeeflix.zip, the latest release.

Copy to your SD card

Extract the zip to the root of your Wii U's SD card. It creates wiiu/apps/coffeeflix.

Launch

Put the SD card back and start CoffeeFlix from the Wii U Menu.

Just the app file? coffeeflix.wuhb goes in sd:/wiiu/apps/coffeeflix/. Older versions and release notes are on the releases page.

Questions

Do I need a YouTube, Google or Twitch account?

No. YouTube, Twitch, radio and podcasts work without signing in. Subscriptions, follows, favorites and what the For you feed learns are saved on your SD card and never leave your console. You can bring your YouTube subscriptions over from Google Takeout or NewPipe in Settings.

Is it free?

Yes. CoffeeFlix is free for personal and other noncommercial use under the PolyForm Noncommercial License. Please don't sell it or bundle it into paid products. If you'd like to support it, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi.

What can it play?

The Wii U decodes H.264 video in hardware, so YouTube, Twitch and most H.264 files play smoothly, with the quality set from the player (360p to 1080p). HEVC, VP9 and MPEG files play in software, best at 480p. Audio: MP3, AAC, FLAC, Vorbis, Opus, ALAC, WAV and more. Subtitles: SRT, ASS, WebVTT and embedded tracks.

How do I watch my own movies?

Put them on the SD card in wiiu/apps/coffeeflix/Videos, or play them from a PC or NAS on your network (My Media → Network shares), a DLNA server, or a Jellyfin server.

How do I update?

Download the latest zip and extract it over the old one. Your settings and subscriptions are kept.

Is the source code available?

Yes, on GitHub. You're welcome to read it, build it and change it for noncommercial purposes.
